How this map works

Matcha Map collects pop-ups that brands post publicly, on their own sites, in event listings and on Instagram, matches each one to a day and a place, and puts them on one map. Dates come from those posts, so check the post before you go. When a brand has not said where, the page says so instead of guessing.

Every pop-up belongs to its brand: Isshiki Matcha, Sunday Culture, Heyteausa, Hey Tea, Partea NYC, Dream Tea NYC, Kinka, Longevity New York, Matcha event, Resident Company Club - Flatiron, Teapulse Nyc, Rhythmzero, Aoko Matcha, Dew Caffeine (NYC Pop-Ups), Gishiki Matcha, KinKa, Matcha Beats Club, Matcha DNA, Matcha House, Matcha Reserve, Matchaful, Plentea Matcha, Senbird Tea, Tenzo Matcha, 12 Matcha, Wild Orchard, The WMatcha Club New York.
