How this map works

Matcha Map collects pop-ups that brands post publicly, on their own sites, in event listings and on Instagram, matches each one to a day and a place, and puts them on one map. Dates come from those posts, so check the post before you go. When a brand has not said where, the page says so instead of guessing.
