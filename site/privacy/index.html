Privacy

The short version

Matcha Map is a free map of matcha pop-ups. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking cookies. We keep as little as we can.

What we keep

  • Forms you send. If you add a pop-up, report a problem, claim a brand or sign up for alerts, what you type is stored in a private database on Cloudflare. That can include the brand name, handles, links, and your email if you gave it. We use it to check the listing and to reply. Pop-up submissions that are not approved are deleted after 30 days.
  • Rate limiting. To stop spam we count form sends per day from an address. We store a one-way hash of the address, not the address itself, for two days.
  • Your device. The map remembers your light or dark choice in your own browser. Nothing is sent to us.

What we do not do

  • No tracking cookies and no ad networks.
  • We do not sell or share your details.
  • We do not log in to anyone's Instagram or TikTok, and we do not scrape behind a login.

Where listings come from

Pop-up details come from public pages, public event listings, open data, and posts that people send us. Instagram information is read only through Instagram's official API, from business and creator accounts that are public. We show a link back to the original post.

Maps and fonts

The map loads tiles and code from third-party servers (MapLibre and OpenStreetMap-based tiles) and the fonts from Google Fonts. Those servers see your IP address when your browser fetches from them.
