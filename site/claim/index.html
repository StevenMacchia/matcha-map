Get your matcha pop-up on the map

Matcha Map is a free map of matcha pop-ups across the US. People use it to find one near them on a given day. If you run pop-ups, cafes or a market, your dates belong on it.

How it works

  1. 1

    Claim your page

    Tell us your brand and your Instagram or TikTok, and your email so we can write back. We check it is really you before anything changes.

  2. 2

    Send your dates

    Post them on your own account as you always do, or send them to us. We read public posts and pages only, never anything behind a login.

  3. 3

    Your pop-up shows up

    On the map, on your city's page and on your own brand page, with your photo and a link back to you.

What you get

  • A page for your brand
  • Your pop-ups in front of people looking in your city that week
  • The right name, place and time, because you can fix them
  • A link back to your account

What it costs

Nothing. (A paid Featured spot exists for brands that want one; it is never needed to be listed.)

What we will not do

We do not sell your data, send you spam, or post for you. We never copy your photos without credit and a link back. If you want your page changed or taken down, say so and it is done that day.

Who it is for

Pop-up brands, cafes that run matcha events, and market and festival organizers with matcha vendors.
