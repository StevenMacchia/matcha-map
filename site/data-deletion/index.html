Remove my data

You can ask us to delete anything we hold about you, or take down a pop-up listing for your brand. It is free and you do not need an account.

How

  1. Email STEVEN_EMAIL_HERE from the address you used on the form, or tell us your brand's Instagram or TikTok handle.
  2. Say what to remove: a form you sent, an email signed up for alerts, or a pop-up or brand page.
  3. We delete it from our database and from the site, and write back to confirm. We aim for 7 days and never take more than 30.

What gets deleted

  • Forms and sign-ups tied to your email or handle, in the private database.
  • Pop-up listings and brand pages you ask us to remove. We also add the account to a do-not-list note so a later scan does not bring it back.

Instagram data

We keep no Instagram login or token for you. Pop-up details we read through Instagram's official API are public post text and links, and are removed the same way. Removing a post on Instagram also removes it from our map on the next refresh.
