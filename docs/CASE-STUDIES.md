# Case studies: how independent pop-ups announce, and what finds them

A running log, one entry per newly found independent matcha pop-up brand (Overture host handles, press reader hits, Yelp leads, the inbox, outreach replies, the recall study). Each entry answers: who, city, how the last three dated pop-ups were announced, which of those channels we can read, which one found the brand for us, and what would have found it a week earlier. At 50 entries the analysis below is written (channels ranked by coverage and readability, adapters to build or tune in order, recall against the 60-brand sample) and again every 50 after that. A finding that changes the finder order goes on the board (LADDER.md) the same day. Channel names follow docs/DISCOVERY-RECALL.md.

Count: **50 of 50** (Saikō, the 30 brands of the first recall sample, and 19 drafted from our own record on 2026-11-24). Analysis below; the next analysis is at 100.

## Are we reading Instagram? (state of 2026-11-23)

What the Graph reads today: business and creator accounts that are on the watch list, through business_discovery (recent posts with caption, date and link), about 180 calls an hour shared by every run on this account (the code's own cap is 200 an hour; one run is limited to 180); hashtag searches (recent and top public posts, captions only) for at most 30 unique hashtags in a rolling 7 days, and that allowance is fully used until about Nov 29. About 180 accounts are being read (the "watching" brands); about 345 handles are marked unreadable.
What it cannot read: personal accounts (the Graph answers "Invalid user id": Saikō Matcha House is one), stories, reels' comments and tags, accounts that are not on the watch list, hashtag posts beyond the cap, location tags, and anything behind a login. TikTok is not read at all except through search snippets. The step that lifts the hashtag cap and widens the read is Meta App Review, which only Steven can start.
Net: the channel that announces first (an independent's own Instagram post or story, and the host venue's tag of it) is mostly not readable today. The readable channels are the ones in the table in docs/DISCOVERY-RECALL.md.
