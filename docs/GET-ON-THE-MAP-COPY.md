# "Get on the map": page copy for the site session

For the one-page explainer the outreach links land on (the site session owns the page and its design; this is the words only). Where it should live is the site session's call: the claim link is `/claim/?brand=<name>&handle=@<handle>`, and the form it opens already prefills from the link, so the explainer can sit just above the form on `/claim/` or on `/promote/` with the same button. Tone: plain and friendly, short, no hype, Mattie allowed. Numbers below are from 2026-11-23; fill them from the data at build time if the page can.

## Words

**Headline:** Get your matcha pop-up on the map

**Lead:** Matcha Map is a free map of matcha pop-ups across the US. People use it to find one near them on a given day. If you run pop-ups, cafes or a market, your dates belong on it.

**How it works (three steps)**
1. Claim your page. Tell us your brand and your Instagram or TikTok, and your email so we can write back. We check it is really you before anything changes.
2. Send your dates. Post them on your own account as you always do, or send them to us. We read public posts and pages only, never anything behind a login.
3. Your pop-up shows up on the map, on your city's page and on your own brand page, with your photo and a link back to you.

**What you get:** a page for your brand; your pop-ups in front of people looking in your city that week; the right name, place and time, because you can fix them; a link back to your account.

**What it costs:** nothing. (A paid Featured spot exists for brands that want one; it is never needed to be listed.)

**What we will not do:** we do not sell your data, send you spam, or post for you. We never copy your photos without credit and a link back. If you want your page changed or taken down, say so and it is done that day (`/data-deletion/`).

**Who it is for:** pop-up brands, cafes that run matcha events, and market and festival organizers with matcha vendors.

**Button:** Claim your page

**Small print under the button:** We write back by email once we have checked it. Questions: reply to the message that brought you here.

## Facts that stay true while it is live

- Claims are read by hand; no claim changes a page until a person has checked it.
- The map lists a brand whether or not it has claimed: claiming adds control, not visibility.
