## What is trending in drinks

Full detail, components and evidence in data/drinks.json (59 drinks, controlled vocabulary at the top). Popularity 4 or 5: Banana matcha, Cloud foam matcha, Iced matcha latte, Strawberry cloud matcha, Strawberry matcha, Ube matcha, Banana pudding matcha, Black sesame matcha, Brown sugar boba matcha, Coconut cloud matcha, Coconut matcha, Gingerbread matcha, Hojicha latte, Hot matcha latte, Mango matcha, Matcha einspanner, Matcha lemonade, Matcha soft serve, Matcha tiramisu, Peppermint or candy cane matcha, Ube cloud matcha, Vanilla sweet cream matcha, Yuzu matcha.

- Fruit layers: strawberry, ube, banana lead. 2026 flavour reports name strawberry, banana and yuzu as the main matcha gainers; ube is the press favorite as "the next matcha".
- Foam tops: cloud and cold foam (strawberry cloud, ube cloud, coconut cloud) are the 2026 topping; Cere Tea in Burlingame lists $9.49 for Strawberry Cloud Matcha.
- Dessert and einspanner formats: banana pudding, tiramisu and einspanner variants, $8.99 to $9.99.
- Holiday (Nov 2026 Instagram menus): peppermint and candy cane matcha, gingerbread, cinnamon cookie butter, sugar plum.
- Prices seen: $6.50 (Brew Point strawberry matcha) to $9.99 (Cere Tea ube and banana pudding); LA Magazine says about $7 is typical.
- Weakly evidenced (popularity 2): sakura, peach, lychee, passionfruit, mint, protein shake, cookies and cream, dalgona. They were on the required list but no source was found.

## Patterns for the discovery engine

How often each kind of source yields public dates (judgement from what was seen, not measured counts):

| Kind | Examples | Public dates | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Holiday markets and fairs | Columbus Circle (Urbanspace), Chinatown holiday market, Old Orchard Fall Festival | High: organizer pages and craft-fair listings carry dates and run for weeks | Vendor lists often appear late; year is sometimes missing (Eventbrite listing above). |
| Cafe residencies and shop takeovers | Filo Cafe November series, Things Between (Josun), Field Theory (Aira) | High: the host posts a dated calendar naming vendors | Match the vendor handle in "Mentions" of the host's post. |
| Brand collabs | MALK x Cha Cha, Rocky's x Saie | Medium: dates announced in press and brand pages, short windows (3 to 4 days) | One-off; low recurrence, high virality. |
| Weekly or monthly market stands | Shogun (Fri and weekends), Mazay (2 to 3 a week), Homies (Tue-Fri cart), The People's Matcha (monthly calendar) | Medium: dates live on Instagram stories and carousels, rarely on websites | Caption search ("this weekend", "Saturday 11/21") is the only reliable route. |
| Aggregators | DMV Matcha Bar weekly roundup, ma.to event pages, Luma | High but delayed | Good second source for dedup. |
| Viral fixed shops | Project Matcha, 12 Matcha, Damo | Not applicable (hours, not dates) | Kind is cafe; show only under the cafe filter. |
